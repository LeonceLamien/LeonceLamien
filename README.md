## 🎓 Research: Master's Thesis

**Predicting Car Collisions from Dashboard Camera Footage**
*Nexar Dashcam Crash Prediction Challenge (Kaggle) · UCLouvain · 2026*

Anticipating collisions in dashcam recordings **0.5, 1 and 1.5 seconds before they happen**, on 1,500 training and 1,344 test real-world videos. I compared **CNN-LSTM, 3D-CNN, ViViT and VideoMAE** architectures. The best approach fine-tunes a **VideoMAE** transformer (self-supervised pre-training on Kinetics-400) on 16-frame clips, combined in an **ensemble** of reproducible models.

- 🏅 **5th out of 237 teams** on the final Kaggle leaderboard
- 📈 **0.849 mAP** (private test set) and **0.827 mAP** (public test set)
- ⚙️ Full pipeline: video ingestion, clip generation, augmentation, fine-tuning, evaluation and ensembling
